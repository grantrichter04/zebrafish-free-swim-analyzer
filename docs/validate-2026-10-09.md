# Checking the 2026-10-09 settings on real sessions

Branch `ccr-922dbe18-vx83fk` changes how the individual measures are computed
(CHANGELOG, "Unreleased"). The choices were made from simulated fish. Before
the branch is merged into `polish/ra-ready`, check them on real recordings.
Nothing is merged or pushed until the owner has seen the results.

## Run

From the repository, in the `freeswim` environment:

```
git fetch origin ccr-922dbe18-vx83fk
git checkout ccr-922dbe18-vx83fk
python -m pytest -q
python scripts/compare_settings.py "<folder of session_ folders>" --csv compare.csv
```

If the sessions are analysed in BL on one scale for the whole experiment, add
`--body-length <px>` with the value shown on Sessions & Units.

## What to check, and what would mean a setting is wrong

1. **Tracker wobble** (the `wob px` column and the summary line). The defaults
   assume 0.5-1.5 px. Much more than 1.5 px means 0.17 s of smoothing is too
   little (try 0.3 s); clearly under 0.3 px means smoothing is barely needed.
   The freeze threshold should sit well above the speed wobble alone produces
   after smoothing: about `wobble_px * 30 / 5 / px_per_BL` BL/s at 30 fps.
2. **Fish left out at 80% tracked.** List them. If good-looking fish drop out,
   the minimum may be too strict for this rig; say how many and their
   tracked %.
3. **Normal swimming barely moves.** For fish swimming at ordinary speed,
   median speed should change by a few percent at most, and distance by a few
   percent (smoothing trims sharp turns slightly). A larger change means
   smoothing is eating real movement.
4. **Freezing becomes plausible.** Old Frz% / FrzN versus new. In the first
   experiment, treated-tank fish 6 sits nearly still for about 4.5 minutes;
   it should now show roughly that as freezing, in one or a few episodes.
   Open that stretch in the Video Inspector and confirm the fish is still.
5. **Straightness** should rise for fish that rest a lot and stay similar for
   fish that swim throughout. `New_StraightnessSeconds_pct` says how much of
   each fish's time it is based on; a very low value means 1 BL/s is too
   high a bar for these fish.
6. **Groups are comparable.** Compare `Tracked_pct` and the "frames with every
   fish tracked" line between groups. A clear difference means distance and
   shoaling distances are biased between them even after the fix.

Also run `python scripts/verify_on_session.py "<folder>"`, the earlier
real-data audit check. If any of its checks fail now, say whether it is
because of the new defaults (e.g. a fish now excluded) or a real regression.

## Report back

the list of excluded fish, the fish 6 result, and for each setting: keep, or
change to what and why. Commit only the report, if asked, and do not push or
